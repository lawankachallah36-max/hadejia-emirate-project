Digital Innovation for
Horse Racing & Equine Commerce

A comprehensive framework for economic transformation, cultural preservation, youth empowerment, and sustainable development — uniting the Emirate's equestrian heritage with modern digital infrastructure.

Read the Vision Explore the Ecosystem

01 — Foundation & Origin

Where the initiative began

The initiative was conceived during a capacity-building workshop in Dutse, Jigawa State — “Capacity Development for 250 Civil Servants on Digital Economy and Digital Transformation,” organized by the Office of the Head of Civil Service and the Ministry of ICT and Digital Economy, in collaboration with NITDA.

That training in digital transformation and economic development inspired a focused question: how could digital solutions preserve cultural heritage while modernizing equine activities across the Hadejia Emirate?

“Tradition and innovation can coexist, complement one another, and together create lasting social, cultural, and economic value.”

02 — Strategic Direction

Vision, mission, and the values that govern them

Vision

A Hadejia Emirate recognized nationally and regionally as a centre of excellence in digital innovation for traditional institutions — preserving equine heritage while creating sustainable opportunity for its people.

Mission

To design, develop, and implement digital solutions that strengthen governance, improve efficiency, promote transparency, preserve heritage, and create sustainable value for every stakeholder in horse racing and equine commerce.

Core Values

Cultural Heritage Preservation Integrity & Accountability Innovation & Excellence Transparency Inclusiveness Sustainability Respect for Traditional Institutions Community Empowerment

04.1 — Digital Registration

Register a horse with the Emirate

This is a working preview of the horse registration module described in the digital ecosystem — the first step toward the centralized equine information system referenced in the proposal.

Preview only — this form runs entirely in your browser and is not yet connected to a live Emirate database. A submitted entry generates a sample registration certificate below, for demonstration.

How registration works

Every horse submitted here would receive a unique registration number, following the format used across the Emirate's records: HDE-HR-YYYY-XXXXXX.

What happens next

In the full system, this record joins the centralized equine database — linking ownership history, veterinary records, race entries, and stable allocation for the same horse.

05 — Implementation

A three-year phased roadmap

PHASE ONE
Foundation

Institutional & Technical Groundwork

  • Institutional approvals and stakeholder consultations
  • System requirements analysis and architecture design
  • Policy and governance framework development
PHASE TWO
Build & Pilot

System Development & Pilot Implementation

  • Software and database development, system integration
  • Pilot implementation with capacity-building and training
  • Public sensitization and performance refinement
PHASE THREE
Scale

Full Deployment & Sustainability

  • Full-scale rollout and continuous system improvement
  • Strategic investment partnerships and service expansion
  • Long-term monitoring, evaluation, and learning

06 — Signature Event

The Hadejia Emirate Horse Racing Festival

A flagship, once-a-year gathering that brings the digital ecosystem to life on the field — where registered horses, verified riders, and the Emirate's racing heritage are celebrated in front of the community, traditional title-holders, and visiting guests.
